Quality attribute motivation are important to avoid redesigning a system.

Systems are redesigned not because of functional requirements but because the system:
- Is not fast enough
- Does not scale
- Is slow to develop
- Not secure
- Hard to maintain

Quality attributes are non functional requiements that describe the qualities of functional requirements and
the overall properties of the system. They do not say what the system does but provide a quality measure on how well the system performs on
a particular dimension
The have direct corellation with the architecture

Example: when user clicks on search button after typing search user gets a list of products that closely match search keywords within at most
100 milliseconds(performance quality)
Store must be available at least 99.9%
Quality requirements do not only satisfy clients but all stakeholders e.g
dev team can deploy a new version at least twice a week

Attribute considerations
- Measurable
Testable
We need to be able measure and test to know if our system meets the requirements
e.g when user clisks on purchase confirmation must be displayed quickly to the user quickly cannot tell if time is fast enough
There is not architecture that can provide all quality attributs
Certain qualities contradict
Some combination of attributes are very hard/impossible to achieve

- Architect need to make tradeoff to get highest chance of success

e.g Login under 1 second vs security (making login slower)

- We need to make sure that system is capable of delivering client requirements
as some requirements may be impossible or prohibitively expensive to achievee.g unrealistic low latency expectations
100% availability hinders maintenance and failure threshold
full protection against hackers, high resolution video streaming in limited bandwidth areas, very high storage growth

Consult domain experts to check feasibility
