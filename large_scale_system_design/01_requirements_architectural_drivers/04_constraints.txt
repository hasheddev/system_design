Once system goals are determined we have freedom on its structure and must make a lot of decisions when defining
the final architecture as there can be multiple ways to ensure the quality attribute is achieved,

A system connstraint is a decision fully or partially made for us limiting our degree of freedom
This might make our job easier and contraints are referred to as pillars because:
- they provide a solid starting point
- System need to be designed around them

Types
- Technical constaints: hardware, language, database, technlogy, os/browser, support
All the above affect design decisions and put restrictions on architecture though they seem to belong to implementation
e.g on premise deployment makes cloud resurces and services are no longer available
support of older browsers or low end mobile affects api and sevices provided

- Business constraints
Architecture and implementation are affected by business constraints like time and budget as some patterns are betetr for smaller and larger organizations
Constaints like third party services with apis and architectural paradigms like 3rd party billing/shipping

- Legal constaints
Global
e.g HIPAA in the US restraints data accessed and stored and how long they can be retained
Regional

Considerations
Take all constaints seriously: We should make distiction between real constaints like rules and regulations and self imposed(negotiable) constraints liek negotaible budget / time
Exploration of hardware, vendor, and technologies

Use loosely coupled architecture e.g db and third party sevice is not tightly coupled to our system such that using a different one takes minimal change
and different system parts should be easily replaced and updated independently
