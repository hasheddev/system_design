Requirements: formal description of what we need to build


Challenges
- Scope and Level of abstraction
- Ambiguity is high as the requirements is often not an engineer or technical

Getting requirements is part of the solution as client does not always know hat thet need but they know what roblem they need to solve


Example: allow people to join drivers on route who are willing to take passengers for a fee
clarifying questions like payment method, user experience(mobile, desktop), realltime vs advance resrvation

If requirements are not gathered we face issues like:
Not being able to easily change the system as it is large
Number of engineers involved wastes engineering resource
Waste of time
Waste of hardware and software cost
Contract and financial obligations

Requirement types
- Features of a system (functional requirements)
- Qualities system (non functional requirements)
- System constraints (limitations and boundaries)

Features: Describe system behaviour and can be easily tied to the system's objectives
users input / events are inputs and the softwares actions are outputs
features do not determine a systems architecture
Examples: when a rider logs into the app the system must display a map with nearby drivers within 5 mile radius
When ride is completed system will charge and credit driver minus service fees

Qualities are properties system must have as opposed to what it must do   like
scalability, performance, reliability, security, availability all which depend on system
Qualities do determine a systems architecture as they determine the system's qualities

Constraints include time, money and engineering resouce available and they are
referred to as architectural driver as the determine architecture that satisfies client needs
