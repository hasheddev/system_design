Monolithic is called because all business logic is in one single service

As size and complexity of codebase grows it becomed difficult to build, test, add new features, trouble shoot and load in our ide
Organization scalability becoms an issue as the more engineers the more code merge conflicts meeting become larger longer and less productive

Microservice is the next step at this stage

Organizes business logic as a collection of loosely coupled and independently deployed services

Each service is owned by a samll team and has a narrow scope of responsibility

- Narrow scope makes code base a lot smaller with faster and easier development, building, testing, add new features, trouble shooting and load in our ide
becomes easier and new developers become more productive faster
- Better performance and horizontal scalability as instances becomes less cou intensive and memory intensive
- High throughput from entire organization as a whole gives organnizational scalability with autonomus decisions on service tecnologies
- Better security due to isolation faults are also isolated and are easier to mitigate

Best practice
Benefits do not come out of the box if we do not follow best practices
Overhead and challenges ave to be considered
- Services must be logically separated such that every change in a service happens in only one service and involves only one team
Use single responsibility principle each service is responsible for one capability, domain, resource or action e.g imager, user, matching, billing
Gateway can be broken down into micor services
- Database separated to avoid coupling of services data must be split to make each service independent but data duplication is expected overhead


Benefits despite overhead and complexity comes only after a certain complexity and organization scale and follwing best practoces on reaching this scale
Start with simple monolithic approach and when it stops workin use microservice
