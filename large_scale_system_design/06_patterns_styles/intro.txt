Architectural patterns are repeatable solutions to commonly occurring system design problems
Solutions to architevtural problems that involve multiple components that run as separate runtime units

Successfull practices from mistakes and what worked from observation by architects of other companies and industries solving similar design problems
become the architectural patterns

Why we use them
- Saves valuable time and resources by using already found solutions
- Avoid making our architecture resembling a big ball of mud where every service talls to every other service and tightly coupled
information is global and duplicated with no clear scope of responsibility for components
This might happen due to rapid growth or lack of well defined architecture and a system in this situation is very hard to develop maintain and scale
- Other engineers can continue working with our system and follow along with it

All patterns are guidelines we choose what is suitable
As system evolves certain architectural pattern may no longer fit our need. The best part of following the patterns is that many companies went through the migrations in the past so we can folllow their
best practices to make migrations quickly and safely
