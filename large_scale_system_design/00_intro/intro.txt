- Motivation

Everything has a structure and more investement in building means it is harder to change
Structure describes intent and qualities of a product
Software architecture gives us infinite ways to otganize our code and different organizations gives us different properties
It impacts the performance  and scale of product, ease of adding new features and response to failure or security attck
Cost of redesign will be significant in terms of time and money for poor arcitecture

- Artitecture Definition
It is a high level description of a system's structure, different components and how they interact with each other to fulfill the
system's requirements and constraints

- Artitecture Definition Explanation
High level: an abstraction that shows important components that help reason about system while hiding implementation e.g technologies and languages
are part of implementation
Components & communication: defined by behaviours and apis that may be complex system with their own architectural diagrams
Requirments & constraints: describes how components do what they must do and must not do

Levels of abstraction
- classes/structs
- modules/packages/libraries
- Services(processes/groups of processses) ***
The more distributed approach allows for the handling of large requests, store and process large amount of data and serve many users per day
e.g ride sharing, social media, banks, video games, video platforms, investment services

Right architecture can mean going from a small startup to a multi billion dollar company
and wrong job in design phase means a waste ot engineering time and system that does not meet requirements needs to berestructured which  is very hard and expensive

- Software Development Cycle
Design, implementation, testing and deployment

Design is the most important and all four phrases may be repeated
Architecture is the output of design and input to implementation phase
Design defines architecture to be implemented and can be challenging as it cannot be proven to be correct / optimal
Hence success is guaranteed by following methodical design process, architectural patterns and industry best practices
