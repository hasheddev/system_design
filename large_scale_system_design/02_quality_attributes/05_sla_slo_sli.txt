an agreement between us, service provider and customer a legal contarct representing quality of service such as availability, perfromance data durability
it states penalty and financial consequencies like full / partial refunds, subscription/license extension
This exists for external paying user, free external users(sometimes) and occasionaly internal users rarely any penalty
For free users this might be dues to issues when usere are using the system with free trial and compensate then with an extension or credits
Cmmpanies that provide free sevices  do not publish slas

SLIs, SLOs, and SLAs are the foundational framework used in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and service management to measure, track, and guarantee service performance and availability.

SLO: service level objectives
individual goals we set for our service likes
availability service slo of 3 nines
response time slo of less than 100ms at the 90th percentile
issue resoluyion time objective of at most 48 hours

Slos include quality attribuutes from beginning of design process and other system objective

Each Slo represent an individual agreement in an sla about a specific metric in the system. An Sla aggregates all the slos in a single legal documents
Systems with no Slas must have slos as without this users do not know what to expect from the system

SLI: service level indicators 
Quantitative measure of complaince with an slo
An actual number measured with monitoring service or calculated from logs
Can be compared to our slos e.g We can measure number of succefull request as sli for availability and compared to
the availability slo of 3 nines that is set


Slos represent taret values for quality attributes highlighting importance of measurability(findning slis to use against slos) and testability
to prove that we meet our slos hence our sla

Sla are crafted by business and legal team while engineers and architects define slos and slis

Consideration
- Do not take every measurable indicator and define an slo for it instead find metrics users care about the most and define slos around them
then find right sli to track those

- Fewer slos is better to be able to proritze them

- Set realistic goals with a budget for error and commit to much lower slosthis saves cost and leaves enough room for unexpected issues
Sometimes separate external slos are separated from internal ones like 99.9 external but 99.99 internal availability to reduce financial culpability

Create recovery plan for when slis show that we are not meeting our slos
e.g system goes down for a long period, performance degrades and we get reports about bugs in the system
The plan should include automatic alerts to engineers, automatic rollbacks, failovers, scaling
predefined handbooks on cituation handling
