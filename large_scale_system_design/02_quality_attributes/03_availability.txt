Availability impact on a system like air control or healthcare determines its importance
as it may affect lives

System going down for too long or frequently means we lose money and users go to competitors

Availability is the fraction of time/probability that our service is oeprationaly functional and accessible to the user

uptime = time system is operationally fuctional and accessible to the user
downtime = time system is unavailable to user
availability = uptime / entire time system is running (uptime + downtime)
availability can sometime be referred to as uptime

Mtbf mean time between failure: average time system is operational useful when dealing with multiple pieces of hardware
and are not using third party service that provide their own metrics

Mttr: average time it takes to detect and recover from failure(average downtime)

Availability = Mtbf + Mtbf + Mttr  this is more useful for estimation
if mttr is zero we have 100% availability though this is not possible

100% availability is not possible as it leaves no time for maintenance and upgrades
 90% availability = 36 day downtime per year
Industry standard is 99.9% = 8h 45m 36s per year = 1 min 26 seconds per day

99.9% = 3 nines 99.99 = four nines
