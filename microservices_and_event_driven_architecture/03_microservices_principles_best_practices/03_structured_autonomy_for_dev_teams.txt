Full team autonomy problems
Misconception/Myth is biggest benefit is each team has full autonomy in choosing tech stack, dbs, frameworks e.t.c this is due to
Upfront cost of infrastructure: amount of effort put nto structuring monolith cannot be put into all microservices
Infrastructure maintenance: Ongoing effort to maintain infrastructure becomes steeper and overwhelming for different service technologies
Steep learning curve: new developer take a lot of time to follow a microservice codebase best practice which may vary for different service making it un scalable
Non uniform api: different communication apis makes frontend and service to service communication a nightmare if different ones are used(learning each api technology)
and different team best practices

For a successful microservices architecture we need balance between autonomy and structure(Structured autonomy)
Each team is autonomous within certain boundaries

3 tiers of development team autonomy

Tier 1 fully restrictive, should be uniform across company e.g
- CI/CD monitoring and alerting making investing time in building this worth it
and cost effective.
-Api guidelines and best practices should be uniform making integration easy
- Security and data compliance as system becomes vulnerable if a service is hacked. Violation of a data policy applies to whole org and not
just the microservice

Tier 2 Freedom with boundaries: programming languages and database technologies as it prevents use of newest technology just for fun and also it takes time to
develop expertise in managing, configuring and running runtimes and dbs in production. Most large companies have a limited number of db technology and languages
used across hundreds of services

Tier 3 Complete autonomy: Includes 
- release process, schedule and frequency so each team can work independently based on their own conditions and customize based on need.
- Documentation
- Custom scripts for local development and testing
- Onboarding process

These tiers are general guidelines

Factors in team autonomy boundary
Size / influence Devops /SRE team
Seniority of developers the more senior the more freedom they prefer
Company culture
