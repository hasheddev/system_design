Introduction Benefits and Challenges of Microservices architecture

Microservices architecture organizes a system as loosely coupled services each owned by a small team and with a narrow scope of responsibility


Benefits
High organization scalability with smaller code base that is quick to load in IDE, easy to reason about, fast to build and test, safer to deploy with smaller and more frequent releases
and speeds up engineer onboarding

High system scalability: Unlike powerful machines needed for monolithic apps microservices consume smaller memory and thus run on cheaper machines and it also allows for teams to use the best
technology for the services while responding to technology changes

High system stability: Impacts on services are isolated to related services


Challenges
Microservices is  a highly distributed system making method calls more unpredictable dues to network communication and each service being unreliable

Testing microservices in isolation is faster but there is no guarantee that they work as a whole leading to slower and complex integration test with blurry ownership

Troubleshooting performance issue and bugs becomes harder

Responsibility boundary becomes harder from organization scalability

These issues can lead to worse situation known as a distributed monolith but there is now established principles and best practices to avoid this.
